capkey

Train your ear with any English video.

Paste a YouTube link or pick a recommended video. capkey plays one sentence, pauses, and gives you a blank for every word. Type what you heard, check it, move on.

Needs a video with English captions (made by the uploader or auto-generated).

This online version can open the recommended videos below, or any video with a transcript you paste. To open any link directly, run capkey on your computer (python server.py).

No captions found? Use your own transcript

Paste an .srt or .vtt file, or the text from YouTube's “Show transcript” panel (lines starting with a time like 0:12). The link above is still used for the video.
